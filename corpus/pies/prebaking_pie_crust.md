# Prebaking (Blind Baking) Pie Crust: When and How

**Category:** Pie baking and troubleshooting  
**Source:** King Arthur Baking, *How to prebake pie crust — and why you should*  
**Author:** PJ Hamel  
**Published:** September 1, 2026  
**Original article:** https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2026/09/01/prebake-blind-bake-pie-crust

## What does prebaking pie crust mean?

Prebaking, also called blind baking, means baking an empty pastry crust before adding its filling. It helps prevent a pale, damp, or undercooked bottom when the filling will not spend enough time in the oven to cook the crust fully.

## When should I prebake a pie crust?

- **Fully prebake** a single crust for a filling that will not be baked afterward, such as banana cream or chocolate cream pie.
- **Partially prebake** a crust if the filling will be baked afterward but needs a relatively short or gentle bake, such as custard pie, pumpkin pie, or quiche, depending on the recipe.
- A fruit pie that bakes for a long time may not require prebaking. Follow the recipe first.
- Prebaking a **single-crust fruit pie** with a crumb topping can help if its bottom repeatedly stays pale. It is less suitable for a double-crust fruit pie because sealing an unbaked top to a prebaked base is difficult.

## How to partially prebake a pie crust

1. Roll out the pastry and fit it into the pie pan. Shape or crimp the edge.
2. **Chill for 30 minutes** to relax the dough and firm its fat, reducing shrinkage.
3. Line the crust with parchment paper, foil, or a suitable paper coffee filter.
4. Fill approximately **two-thirds** of the lined crust with pie weights, dried beans, uncooked rice, other dry grains, or granulated sugar. The weights limit bubbling and slumping.
5. Heat the oven to **375°F (about 190°C)**. If available, preheat a baking stone or steel and set the pie on it.
6. Bake for **20 minutes**. Remove the crust and carefully lift out the lining and weights.
7. At this point the crust is **partially prebaked** and can receive a filling that will finish baking in the oven.

## How to fully prebake a pie crust

After the initial 20-minute weighted bake:

1. Remove the lining and weights.
2. Prick the bottom of the crust with a fork to discourage bubbling.
3. Return it to the **375°F (about 190°C)** oven for another **20–25 minutes**, until golden throughout.
4. Let it cool as appropriate before adding a filling that will not be baked.

These times are guidance from the source article; follow a particular recipe's instructions when they differ.

## Why did my crust shrink or slide down the pan?

Pastry can contract as it heats, especially if it has not been chilled. First try the 30-minute chill and sufficient pie weights. If the sides still slip, the source suggests an **upside-down two-pan method**:

1. Use two identical or closely nesting pie pans with flat rims.
2. Place the pastry in the first pan, secure the rim with fork marks, add a liner, and nest the second pan over it.
3. Turn both pans upside down onto a baking sheet and bake for about **20 minutes** as for the weighted method.
4. Turn them right-side up carefully, remove the second pan and liner, then finish baking the crust upright if a fully baked shell is needed.

The upside-down arrangement uses gravity to keep the pastry toward the rim rather than letting it slump into the bottom.

## Can I prebake a fruit pie crust?

Yes, particularly for a **single-crust** fruit pie with a streusel or crumb topping. A prebaked crust is harder to seal to an uncooked top crust, and juices may leak out. Shield the exposed rim during the filled bake so it does not overbrown.

## Quick troubleshooting questions

### Why is my pie crust soggy even though the filling is cooked?

The filling may cook faster than the pastry base. Consider partial prebaking when the recipe and pie type allow it.

### Do I need to prebake chocolate cream pie crust?

Yes. Because the filling is cooked before being added and the assembled pie is not baked, the pastry crust needs to be fully prebaked.

### Do I need to prebake pumpkin or custard pie crust?

Partial prebaking can help the bottom brown without overcooking the delicate filling, though some recipes use other methods and do not require it.

### How long do I chill the crust before prebaking?

The source recommends **30 minutes** before placing the weighted crust in the oven.

### What oven temperature and baking time should I use?

The source's general method is **375°F / about 190°C for 20 minutes with weights**; for a fully baked crust, remove the weights and continue for **20–25 minutes** more until golden.

### What if the crust slides down even with weights?

Try the nested two-pan upside-down method described above.
